About MRD Benefits Consulting

We believe great benefits shouldn’t be complicated. MRD Benefits Consulting was founded to give businesses and families the kind of personal, straightforward advice usually reserved for large corporations.

We take time to understand your goals, explain your options in plain language, and stay with you long after enrollment ends.

  • Independent advice. Recommendations based on your needs, not a sales quota.
  • Local & personal. A real person you can call, based in Aurora, CO.
  • Year-round support. Help with claims, changes, and renewals — not just enrollment.
