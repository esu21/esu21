Why start preparing at 55?

Starting at age 55 gives you about seven years before age 62 to get ready. During that time you can review your Social Security earnings record, ask SSA to correct possible errors, learn how work credits are earned, and understand how additional work may affect future eligibility.

A worker can earn no more than four Social Security credits per year, so seven years can add at most 28 new credits. Preparation does not guarantee eligibility or a particular benefit amount — but it helps you go into the process informed.
