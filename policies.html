Disclaimer

MRD Benefits Consulting provides general benefits education, document organization, and preparation assistance. MRD does not sell, solicit, negotiate, endorse, or recommend insurance products and does not enroll clients in Medicare plans. MRD is an independent private business. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of the Social Security Administration, Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services, Medicare, any insurance company, or any government agency. MRD does not determine eligibility, calculate an official benefit, promise an outcome, or provide legal, tax, financial, medical, or insurance advice. Only the responsible government agency, plan, or other authorized organization can make an official decision.

Retirement education

Retirement information is general education. MRD does not calculate an official benefit, recommend a claiming age, assess an investment strategy, give tax advice, correct an earnings record for the client, or act as an SSA representative. Clients should confirm information with SSA and consult licensed financial, tax, or legal professionals when appropriate.

SSI and SSDI organization

MRD provides organization and general process education only. MRD does not determine disability, evaluate medical sufficiency, prepare legal arguments, act as an appointed representative, appear at a hearing, sign or submit information as the client, or receive payment from past-due benefits. Formal representation before the Social Security Administration requires registration as an appointed representative and an SSA-approved fee agreement or fee petition; MRD’s flat SSI or SSDI Coaching and Application Preparation fee is charged only for coaching and organization work performed outside that representation process.

Service area

MRD offers remote educational and administrative-support appointments only where the requested service is permitted by applicable law and falls within MRD’s approved service scope. MRD may decline or limit a service based on the client’s state of residence, the nature of the question, licensing requirements, or the need for a licensed or authorized professional.

Clients remain responsible for their decisions, submissions, and deadlines and should confirm program information with the responsible agency or an appropriately licensed or authorized professional.

Privacy

  • We never ask for your Social Security number, Medicare number, passwords, PINs, security answers, or government-account login.
  • We do not accept complete medical records — only the redacted pages needed for your service.
  • Please do not send sensitive documents by regular email or text message. Email and text are for scheduling only. Accepted documents are shared through a secure portal.
  • Phone and video sessions are never recorded, and automatic transcription and AI meeting summaries are turned off.
  • We do not put identifiable client information into public AI tools.
  • Access to client files is limited to the owner. Routine service files are kept for three years after your file is closed, then securely deleted.
  • If you send us sensitive information we didn’t need, we will isolate and securely delete it and ask for a redacted copy.

Cancellation, refunds, and fees

  • Free consultation: Every engagement begins with a free 15–30 minute consultation by phone, Microsoft Teams, or Zoom.
  • Deposit to book: $70 for the Medicare Education Session (Regular), $90 for the Medicare Education Session (SEP), $120 for the Retirement Education Session, $190 for the Combined Medicare SEP and Retirement Package, or $70 for the SSI or SSDI Coaching Package. The balance is due at or before your appointment.
  • Missed or unscheduled appointments: If you do not schedule your paid appointment after paying the deposit, or do not attend it, the deposit is not refundable.
  • If MRD cancels, you may choose a full refund or rescheduling.
  • Outside our scope: If screening shows your matter is outside our scope before substantive work begins, you receive a full refund.
  • After preparation begins, any refund equals the amount paid minus documented completed work and nonrefundable direct costs.
  • No refund is based solely on a government, plan, insurer, or benefit outcome that MRD does not control.
  • Travel: Any in-person travel fee is disclosed in writing and approved by you in advance.
  • Interpretation: Professional interpretation is arranged in advance, and its cost is disclosed and approved by you before service. Family members may interpret only with your informed consent.
  • Statement of work: At the end of every engagement, you receive a written statement summarizing the services performed.
  • Applicable state consumer law controls if it gives you greater rights.

Accessibility

Accessibility is central to how MRD works. This website is built to work with keyboards and screen readers such as JAWS, and every service includes:

  • Your choice of telephone, accessible Microsoft Teams, or Zoom appointments
  • Keyboard- and screen-reader-accessible Word summaries
  • Large print on request
  • Other reasonable accommodations arranged before your appointment
