About MRD Benefits Consulting

Helping people understand complicated benefits processes through accurate education, respectful guidance, accessible communication, and practical organization.

Meet the founder

Esubalew Ethan Johnston is the founder and owner of MRD Benefits Consulting LLC.

Ethan worked as a Social Security Administration Claims Specialist from September 2014 through January 2020. His public service included recognition with an SSA Commissioner’s Award connected with COVID-19 Medicare work. Today he works in healthcare technology accessibility and consulting.

That experience shapes how MRD works: clear explanations, careful organization, and services designed from the start to be accessible to everyone.

Ethan’s former SSA employment does not give MRD any government authority. MRD is a private business and is not affiliated with or endorsed by SSA or any government agency.

Our values

  • Dignity and patient communication for every client.
  • Neutrality and independence from insurance commissions.
  • Plain language and accessible written materials.
  • Privacy — we collect only the minimum information needed.
  • Honest boundaries and prompt referrals.

Our vision

To become a trusted, accessibility-centered source of neutral benefits education for older adults, people with disabilities, survivors, families, and underserved communities.

Where we serve

MRD offers remote appointments by phone, accessible Microsoft Teams, or Zoom. We are starting with clients in Colorado and will add other states as each is reviewed and approved. We may decline or limit a service based on your state, the nature of your question, or licensing requirements. During your free consultation we will ask where you will be located during the appointment.
