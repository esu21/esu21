Services & Pricing

Each service has a fixed written scope so you know exactly what is included and what it costs. Every engagement starts with a free 15–30 minute consultation.

At a glance

  • Retirement Education Session — $225
  • Medicare Education Session — Regular or On-Time Enrollment — $125
  • Medicare Education Session — Special Enrollment Period (SEP) — $175
  • Combined Medicare SEP and Retirement Package — $375
  • Survivor Education Session — $449
  • SSI or SSDI Coaching and Application Preparation — $125
  • Other Approved Assistance — $175 per hour

Included with every session

  • Accessibility: Telephone, accessible Microsoft Teams, or Zoom appointment; keyboard- and screen-reader-accessible Word summary; large print on request; other reasonable accommodations arranged in advance.
  • Interpretation: Arranged before your appointment. Any professional interpreter cost is disclosed and approved by you in writing.
  • Privacy: Sessions are never recorded or transcribed. We never ask for your Social Security number, Medicare number, or passwords.

Community Access Program

Clients with household income at or below 200% of the federal poverty guideline, or who are experiencing documented financial hardship, may request a 50% fee reduction. During our first year, up to two reduced-fee appointments are available each month, and availability is not guaranteed. Reduced-fee clients receive the same service scope and quality.

Ask about the program during your free consultation.

Workshops for organizations

Churches, nonprofits, employers, disability organizations, senior organizations, and community groups can book general educational workshops on Social Security and benefits preparation. Workshops are educational only — no individual sales or plan advice. Contact us to discuss your group.

What MRD does not do

  • Sell, recommend, rank, or compare named insurance plans for purchase
  • Enroll you in Medicare Advantage, Part D, Medigap, or any insurance
  • Accept insurance commissions or paid referrals
  • Determine eligibility or calculate an official benefit
  • Represent you before SSA, handle appeals or hearings, or take payment from past-due benefits
  • Give legal, tax, investment, financial-planning, or medical advice
  • Guarantee any outcome

If your question involves a denial, appeal, overpayment, urgent deadline, or choosing a specific plan, we will refer you to the right agency or licensed professional — such as SSA, Medicare, your State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP), or an attorney.
